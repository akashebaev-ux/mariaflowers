About Maria Flowers

Who We Are

Maria Flowers is an online flower marketplace that works with carefully selected flower shops to offer customers consistently high-quality bouquets and floral arrangements.

We do not limit our customers to the selection of one florist. Instead, we work with trusted partner flower shops and select the most suitable florist for each order based on quality, freshness, availability and delivery requirements.

What We Bring to the Table

We do not simply sell flowers.

We pick the best of the best from our network of partner flower shops.

Our role is to make sure that customers do not have to search through dozens of florists, compare quality or worry about whether the bouquet will meet their expectations.

Maria Flowers does that work for you.
